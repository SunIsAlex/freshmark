About this place

A blog that respects your attention.

Mathematical proofs, physical intuition, chemical reactions, and practical technology. A collection of questions worth taking your time with.

The idea

PrinciplesSay hello

This blog is built around a simple belief: reading on the web should feel clear, warm, and unhurried. There are no ads, pop-ups, or third-party trackers; optional same-origin analytics store aggregate numbers, account emails are never public, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

Principles

  • Typography carries the design.
  • Every feature should earn its place.
  • Good defaults make publishing easy.
  • The site should remain pleasant on any screen.
